Home / Museum Ship Reports / International Maritimes Museum, Hamburg

Location: Internationales Maritimes Museum, Kaispeicher B, Koreastraße 1, 20457 Hamburg, Germany

Visited: 15 April 2023

After taking a few days out to drive from one side of the continent to the other, I got back into the swing of things today.

First stop was the International Maritimes Museum in Hamburg.

This museum is pretty extensive - spreading over nine floors, and includes one of the largest collections of ship models I have come across.  They also had two complete German midget submarines from WW2 - a Molch and a Seehund.  I have seen a few Seehund's before, but this was my first Molch (although apparently there is another one in a museum in Johannesburg, South Africa!).

The other highlight for me was the bookshop - which had a really good selection of second-hand books in both English and German.  Unfortunately my purchasing capacity was limited by my ability to carry the books back to the hotel via public transport, but I have now got long sought-after copies of R&R's books on British Battleships and British Cruisers of WW2, plus Die Deutschen Kriegsschiffe - which is the size and weight of a small windowless building - god knows how I am going to fit them into my luggage allowance to get home - but that is a problem for another day.

Another museum well worth the visit.
