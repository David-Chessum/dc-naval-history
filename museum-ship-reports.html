About the Reports

The linked pages are based on a series of naval museum reports originally published online in the NAVWEAPS forum and/or the MuseumShips Facebook group.

All photographs in these reports are © the author unless otherwise specified.

I do not mind photographs being reproduced provided credit is given. However, it is courteous to ask first, and I probably have higher resolution versions and/or additional images available for most of the museums upon request.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if there are any corrections or updates to information contained in the reports.
