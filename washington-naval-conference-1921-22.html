Documents of the Washington Naval Conference, 1921-22

The WNC was one of the major diplomatic events of the interwar period. Senior delegations from the victorious nations of World War One gathered to discuss naval disarmament and the resolution of outstanding Far Eastern problems. The Conference resulted in several treaties and resolutions, the most significant of which was the Treaty Limiting Naval Armament.
