Japanese Capital Ship Guns Reused for Coastal Defence

The Washington Naval Treaty required Japan to dispose of a large number of capital ships - both new ones under construction, and older vessels. Many of the guns from these ships were re-used as coastal defence batteries as shown in the following table:

ShipGunsLocation
TosaTwin 16” turretsToyo Battery, Kunoshita Saki, Tsushima Island (A Turret) Chosito Battery, Pusan, Korea (B Turret)
AkagiTwin 16” turretKurosaki battery, Iki Shima (A Turret)
KashimaTwin 12” turretsChiyoga Saki battery, Tokyo Bay (Fore Turret) Azuchioshima battery, Iki Shima (After Turret)
IbukiTwin 12” turretsOmazaki battery, Tsugaru (Fore Turret) Tsurumi Saki battery, Houyo Fortress, Kyushu (After Turret)
SettsuTwin 12” turretsTatsuno Saki battery, Tsushima (both 50 cal turrets)
IkomaTwin 12” turretSuzaki battery, Tokyo Bay (fore turret)
AkiTwin 10” turretsJagoshima battery, Tokyo Bay (Two Turrets)
KuramaTwin 8” turretsDaibusamisaki battery, Tokyo Bay (Two Turrets)
