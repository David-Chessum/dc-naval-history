Home / Museum Ship Reports / Danish War Museum, Copenhagen

Date visited: 5 April 2024

This museum is in central Copenhagen in the building of the original arsenal.  The ground floor has a large collection of cannons, while the top floor is split between galleries on Denmark at War, and the Danish Navy.  The Danish Navy gallery has a remarkable collection of ship models - including many very large and intricate models of sailing ships.  From the modern era, there were many models also, including models of most of the coast-defence ironclads built by Denmark around the turn of last century.

The museum shop had a good collection of books - all in Danish - including some published by the museum that I hadn't seen elsewhere.

Worth a visit if you are in town.
