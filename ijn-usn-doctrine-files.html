IJN/USN Doctrine Files

W. David Dickson has been called “the foremost Western expert on Japanese naval doctrine and carrier design” and was also an authority on communications and tactics. He was the author of The Battle of the Philippine Sea, and co-edited On Seas Contested, and To Crown the Waves. David passed away in 2017, and is missed by those in the naval history community who knew him.

The following documents are from David’s collection.
