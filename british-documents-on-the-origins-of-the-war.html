British Documents on the Origins of the War

British Documents on the Origins of the War, 1898-1914 is the official published collection of selected British diplomatic documents concerning the international developments leading up to World War I. Drawing on Foreign Office records, the collection documents Britain’s emergence from diplomatic isolation, the formation of alliances and understandings, and the successive international crises that shaped British foreign policy before 1914.

The collection comprises eleven numbered volumes, with Volumes IX and X each divided into two parts. These are organised around major diplomatic questions, including the Anglo-Japanese Alliance, relations with France and Russia, Anglo-German tensions, the Moroccan crises and developments in the Balkans and Near East. Volume XI, The Outbreak of War, covers the final crisis of 1914.

The collection provides an important primary source for studying the diplomatic origins of the war.

The volumes are all available online via Archive.Org, and copies have been linked in the listing below.
