Home / Museum Ship Reports / German Navy Memorial (Deutscher Marinebund), Laboe

Address: Strandstraße 92, 24235 Laboe, Germany

Date visited 16 April 2023

First visit today was the German Navy Memorial (Deutscher Marinebund) at Laboe - about 20 minutes from Kiel, home of the U-995 - the last surviving Type VII submarine.  The submarine is in immaculate condition, and the quality of workmanship evident in her construction is outstanding - she was much better built than the post-war German submarine I visited later in the day.

The memorial itself is both impressive and sobering.  There is a history hall there with displays and ship models featuring German naval history from the founding of Germany to the present day.  There is a large subterranean hall containing wreaths and memorials for various units/ships/events, and then the tower - the bottom few floors of the tower contain a variety of memorials/artefacts, and then you can catch an elevator to the top - given how flat the topography is up there, you would be able to see a very long way on a clear day - and even on a day like today, I could still see bits of Denmark.

The museum shop is also to be commended - they had quite a range of books and naval magazines - many of which I hadn't seen before.

Everything about this memorial is well done, and I highly recommend it.
