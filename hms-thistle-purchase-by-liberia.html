HMS Thistle – Purchase by Liberia

HMS Thistle was a first-class gunboat of 710 tons built in 1899, and in 1922 was armed with two 4-inch guns and two 3-pdrs. From 1919-24 Thistle operated in West African waters. In 1924, it was proposed to sell Thistle to the Liberian government. The proposal was rejected by the Admiralty on the grounds that it would be a breach of the Washington Naval Treaty. Thistle was ultimately sold for scrapping in 1926.
