Treaties and Resolutions approved and adopted by the Conference on the Limitation of Armament, Washington, 21 November 1921 – 6 February 1922

Treaties

1. Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France, Italy and Japan limiting Naval Armament.

2. Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France, Italy and Japan relating to the use of Submarines and Noxious Gases in Warfare.

3. Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France and Japan relating to their Insular Possessions and Insular Dominions in the Region of the Pacific Ocean.

4. Declaration on the part of the United States of America, the British Empire, France and Japan accompanying the Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France and Japan relating to their Insular Possessions and Insular Dominions in the Region of the Pacific Ocean.

5. Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France and Japan supplementary to the Treaty between the United States of America, the British Empire, France and Japan relating to their Insular Possessions and Insular Dominions in the Region of the Pacific Ocean.

6. Treaty between the United States of America, Belgium, the British Empire, China, France, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands and Portugal relating to Principles and Policies concerning China.

7. Treaty between the United States of America, Belgium, the British Empire, China, France, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands and Portugal relating to the Chinese Customs Tariff.

Resolutions

1. Resolution establishing a Commission of Jurists to consider Amendment of the Laws of War.

2. Resolution limiting the Jurisdiction of the Commission of Jurists.

3. Resolution regarding the Sale of Ships before the Ratification of the Treaty limiting Naval Armament.

4. Resolution establishing a Board of Reference for Far Eastern Questions.

5. Resolutions regarding Extraterritoriality in China.

6. Resolution regarding Foreign Postal Agencies in China.

7. Resolution regarding Foreign Armed Forces in China.

8. Resolution regarding Radio Stations in China, and accompanying Declarations.

9. Resolution regarding the Unification of Railways in China, and accompanying Declaration by China.

10. Resolution regarding the Reduction of Chinese Military Forces.

11. Resolution regarding Existing Commitments of China or with respect to China.

12. Resolution regarding the Chinese Eastern Railway, approved by the Powers including China.

13. Resolution regarding the Chinese Eastern Railway, approved by the Powers other than China.
