Argentine Procurement of ex-German Minesweepers

On 13 February 1922, the Argentine Government decided at a cabinet meeting to acquire 18 auxiliary vessels, ten of 500 tons displacement for the use of the navy, hydrographic and lighthouse services and salvage service, and eight of 185 tons displacement for river services. An offer from the German firm of Hugo Stinnes was accepted within days. The unit price was 43,300 pesos (cUSD$18,400) for the larger vessels and approximately 30,000 pesos (cUSD$12,738) for the smaller vessels. The ten larger vessels supplied were the ex-German ‘M’ type minesweepers listed in the following table, which were not apparently purchased by Hugo Stinnes until 18 Mar 1922 - a month after their offer to the Argentine Government had been accepted. The minesweepers crossed the Atlantic with the merchant ship Fritz Hugo Stinnes V as their support vessel.
