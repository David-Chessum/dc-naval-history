Preserved Warships

The following table is a list of preserved warships dating from c1860 onwards that are known to exist, including replicas and remains where considered relevant. The table also provides links to relevant naval museum reports on this site, as well as links to the applicable MuseumShips.us pages, and the museum websites (where known).

This is a dynamic list, as warship museums are being regularly opened, closed or moved around the world, and there is a regrettably regular procession of former museum ships to the disposal yards – in just the last few years we have lost Minsk, Clamagore, Otama, Hiddensee and several others.

Corrections and additions welcome

If you identify any errors or omissions in this table, I would be pleased to hear from you. Please use the Contact page.
