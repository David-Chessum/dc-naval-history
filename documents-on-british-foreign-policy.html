Documents on British Foreign Policy

Documents on British Foreign Policy, 1919-1939 (DBFP) is the official published collection of selected British diplomatic documents covering the interwar period. It comprises 64 volumes published between 1946 and 1986. Drawing principally on Foreign Office records, the collection makes available contemporary correspondence, telegrams, memoranda and records of diplomatic discussions concerning the formulation and conduct of British foreign policy between the aftermath of the First World War and the outbreak of the Second.

Individual volumes concentrate on particular regions, international conferences or diplomatic questions, rather than providing a single chronological narrative. The collection is arranged in four series:

  • Series 1: 1919-25
  • Series 1A: 1925-30
  • Series 2: 1929-38
  • Series 3: 1938-39

Most of the volumes are available online via Archive.org, and the individual volumes in the list below have been hyperlinked to an Archive.org copy where one has been identified. In the event of broken links, or if you locate an online copy of one that I have missed, please use the contact page.
