Ex-German Minesweepers for Albania

In 1926 it was reported by N.I.D. that two ex-German warships “had been purchased from Italy by Albania and named Shqipnia and Skanderbeg. In the absence of information relating to the exact type of vessel involved, or their former names in German service, the Admiralty advised against raising objections, on the grounds that it might invite objections to the British practice of not considering trawlers used as war vessels as being precluded from transfer by the WNT.

The two ships were in fact the ex-German minesweepers FM16 and FM23. These vessels are reported to have been sold to Romania for commercial service on 10 March 1920. On 25 December 1925, the Albanian Parliament approved funding for the purchase from Italy of the two vessels. The two vessels arrived from Italy on 14 February 1926 (Shqipnia) and 1 March 1926 (Skanderbeg) and were commissioned into the Albanian Navy.
