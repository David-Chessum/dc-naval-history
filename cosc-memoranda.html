Chiefs of Staff Committee Memoranda

The Chiefs of Staff Committee (COSC) was established in 1923 as a sub-committee of Britain’s Committee of Imperial Defence. It brought together the First Sea Lord, the Chief of the Imperial General Staff and the Chief of the Air Staff to co-ordinate planning across the Royal Navy, the Army and the Royal Air Force and to advise the government on imperial defence. It was the principal forum in which the professional heads of the three services considered common strategic problems and agreed recommendations.

The Chiefs of Staff Committee Memoranda are a series of numbered papers circulated for the committee’s consideration. They include strategic appreciations, reports from the Joint Planning Sub-Committee, submissions from individual services and correspondence on subjects such as the defence of Singapore and Hong Kong, the protection of seaborne trade, naval treaty restrictions and preparations for a possible war with Germany.
