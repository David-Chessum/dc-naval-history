Home / Museum Ship Reports / Deutsches Technikmuseum, Berlin

Location: Deutsches Technikmuseum, Trebbiner Str. 9, 10963 Berlin, Germany

Visited: 9 September 2025

We came across one preserved naval vessel in Berlin - the Deutsches Technikmuseum Berlin have a large and varied collection which includes a Biber midget submarine.  Other naval artefacts on display include a chunk of armour from Seydlitz and a life-ring from the battleship Baden.
