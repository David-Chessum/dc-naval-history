Brassey's Naval Annual

The Naval Annual (retitled Brassey’s Naval and Shipping Annual from 1920 to 1936) is a British reference work which combines reviews of the Royal Navy and foreign fleets with articles on naval policy, warship design, shipbuilding and merchant shipping, alongside tables of ship characteristics.
