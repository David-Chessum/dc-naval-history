Plans de Bateaux

In approximately 2005-2006, the French Ministère de la Défense (Ministry of Defence) published a large number of ship plans covering many French warships from the late 19th Century through to the early 1970s. These were subsequently removed from the Internet following a hacking incident, and while a subset of the plans is currently available on the Dreadnought Project site, it is not believed that the full set is currently accessible online.

Fortunately I managed to download copies of most of the plans before they were removed. The table below provides a full index of the plans originally published, and indicates which ones I have copies of.
