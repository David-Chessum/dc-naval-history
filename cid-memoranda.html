Committee of Imperial Defence Memoranda

The Committee of Imperial Defence (CID) was established in 1902 to provide a permanent framework for considering the defence of the United Kingdom and the British Empire. Chaired by the Prime Minister, it brought together ministers, senior service officers and other experts according to the subjects under discussion. It was a consultative and advisory body with no executive powers, its role was to examine defence questions and recommend policy to the Cabinet, rather than direct the armed forces or administer government departments. It continued until September 1939, when its functions were assumed by the War Cabinet.

CID Minutes and Memoranda were issued in a number of series:

  • Home Defence (A Series)
  • Miscellaneous (B Series)
  • Colonial Defence (C Series)
  • Indian Defence (D Series)
  • Overseas Defence (M Series and ODC Series)
  • Overseas Defence Committee Remarks (R Series)
  • Home Ports Defence Committee / Home Defence Committee Minutes (HPDC / HDC Series)
  • Air Committee Minutes and Memoranda (AC and AP Series)
  • Standing Defence Sub-Committee Memoranda (SS Series)
  • Air Raids Precautions Committee Minutes and Memoranda (ARP Series)
  • Advisory Committee on Trading and Blockade in Time of War and Advisory Committee on Trade Questions in Time of War Minutes and Memoranda (ATB Series)
  • Sub-Committee on Industrial Intelligence in Foreign Countries Minutes and Memoranda (IFC and FCI Series)
  • Standing Inter-Departmental Committee on censorship Minutes and Memoranda (SCC and other Series)
  • War Emergency Legislation committee and War Legislation Committee Minutes and Memoranda (WEL and WL Series)
  • Joint Intelligence Sub-Committee Minutes and Memoranda (JIC, JIC(S), JIC(A) Series)
  • Standing Inter-Departmental Committee on National Service in a Future War and Sub-committee on Manpower Minutes and Memoranda (NS and MED Series)
  • Principal Supply Officers committee Minutes and Memoranda (PSO Series)
  • Minister for the Co-ordination of Defence Registered Files (CD Series)
