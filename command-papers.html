Command Papers

UK Parliamentary Command Papers are a long-running series of official documents presented to Parliament “by Command of His/Her Majesty”. In practical terms, they are one of the principal ways in which the British government has published policy statements, reports, treaty texts, and the findings of committees and commissions for parliamentary scrutiny and public record.

The following index lists Command Papers relevant to my areas of study that I have acquired copies of. Links are provided where copies of the papers are available online.

Corrections and additions welcome

If you can identify relevant Command Papers that are missing from these lists, or provide corrections or additional information, please use the Contact page.
