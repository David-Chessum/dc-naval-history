Home / Museum Ship Reports / Narvik War Museum

The Narvik War Museum is a very well presented museum with a lot of interesting artefacts. There is a gun and several other salvaged objects from the Coast Defence Ship Norge, and a large animated diorama showing the naval battles of Narvik, with the various ships moving around, and commentary in Norwegian.  There is also about half of a Marder human torpedo (i.e. they have the front and rear sections of it, but no middle!) - which is complete enough for me to count it as museum ship No. 211. A minor criticism is that some of the artefacts seem to be somewhat randomly distributed around the museum.  Many of the artefacts had bilingual labels - but not all, and some of the labels didn't seem to be that helpful - e.g. the one for the human torpedo had very little information.
