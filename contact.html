Contact

I welcome correspondence from researchers and others interested in naval history, particularly interwar naval arms control, warship construction, warship exports (new and second hand), current museum ships, and the subjects covered elsewhere on this website.

I would particularly welcome corrections or additions to the information presented on this site, information about sources I may not have encountered, and enquiries concerning documents in my research collection.

For further information on any of the material posted on this site, or if you would like to exchange information or documents, please contact me using one of the methods below.
